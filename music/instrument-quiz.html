🎻 Instruments & Terms Quiz

20 true/false questions · no time limit · instruments, clefs, terms & voices.

🎻 Ready? Here's how it works.

Quiz rules
  • 20 true/false questions per round. There is no time limit - take your time.
  • You can't pick the question type. All kinds below are mixed together at random.
  • Read the statement, tap True or False (it turns blue), then press Submit (or Enter).
  • You can change your mind before pressing Submit. You can't go back once submitted.
  • At the end you'll get a score and a full question-by-question review.
What kind of statements?
  • A term's meaning - e.g. "Arco means to play with the bow."
  • A percussion instrument's pitch - definite or indefinite.
  • An instrument's family - string / brass / woodwind.
  • Whether an instrument is transposing or non-transposing.
  • Which instrument has a higher range within a family.
  • The highest / lowest sounding instrument in a family.
  • Single vs double reed instruments.
  • Which voice has a higher range (soprano … bass).
  • Which clef an instrument uses.
  • Whether a term (e.g. con sord., una corda, pizzicato) can be used on a given instrument.
  • Whether an instrument can only play one note at a time.
How to answer

Tap True or False - your pick turns blue - then press Submit (or press Enter). The Submit button stays disabled until you've picked one.
