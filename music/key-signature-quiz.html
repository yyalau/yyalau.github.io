🎼 Key Signature Quiz

20 random questions · 10-40s per question · major & minor, sharps & flats.

🎼 Ready? Here's how it works.

Quiz rules
  • 20 questions per round, drawn from the question types you pick below.
  • Pick the time per question (10-40s) - the bar shows time left (green -> yellow -> red).
  • Tap your answer, then press Next (key-name) or Submit (key-signature). Picked but didn't press? Your selection is still saved when time runs out.
  • No selection in time? It counts as missing and moves on by itself.
  • You can't go back. At the end you'll get a score and a full question-by-question review.
Three question styles
  • Signature -> key: "If a key has 3 sharps (F♯ C♯ G♯), the major key is ____" → e.g. answer: A
  • Key -> signature: "If it is A major, its key signature is ____" → e.g. answer: tap F♯ C♯ G♯
  • Relative keys: "What is the relative minor of C major?" → e.g. answer: a
  • Both major and minor keys can show up in any style.
How to answer

Key-name questions (find the key, or find the relative key): tap the one key name you think is right - it turns blue - then press Next (or Enter).

Key-signature questions (find the sharps/flats): tap every sharp or flat that belongs to the key - they turn blue - then press Submit (or Enter). Pick the right set; the order you tap them in doesn't matter.

Short on time? If you've picked an answer but don't press Next/Submit before the clock runs out, your selection is still recorded. Only a fully blank answer counts as missing.

Names use ♯ for sharp and ♭ for flat (e.g. F♯, B♭). The choices are listed in alphabetical order within each row. Tap None for keys with no accidentals (C major / a minor).
